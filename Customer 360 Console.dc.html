Design note · v1 · for review

Customer 360 — Delivery Assurance Console

One console for a customer success team running a portfolio of contracted engagements. Built around a single question asked in two directions: is this client on track, and which of my other clients has the same problem. Light theme is the default; the ◐ button in the top bar flips to dark.

What I assumed
  • Professional services delivery — fixed-price call-offs, T&M, and managed run under framework agreements.
  • "Smart contract" = machine-checkable clauses (milestone gates, SLA credits, change control) evaluated automatically against delivery evidence.
  • Primary user is a CSM covering 8–15 accounts; secondary is the delivery partner prepping a QBR.
  • Read-only against source systems; risks and journal entries are authored here.
Where the data comes from
  • Salesforce — account, contract instruments, ACV/TCV, change orders, renewal, CSAT.
  • Workday — assignments, utilisation, open roles, attrition, burn, margin.
  • Jira — epics, velocity, blocked age, release evidence for gate verification.
  • Every figure carries a source badge, so any QBR claim traces back in one click.
The design bet
  • Lead with outcomes and clauses, not activity. A green burndown beside a breached gate is the failure this fixes.
  • The customer overview is a triage surface: rank by exposure, act without opening the account.
  • Correlation is a first-class view — shared services mean shared blast radius.
  • Dense instrument panel on paper: a screen someone keeps open all day.
